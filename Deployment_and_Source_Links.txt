SUGGESTED WALKTHROUGH FOR ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open the live application. The public archive requires no login and lists
   four papers published through the full editorial lifecycle.

2. Open any paper to see its record and citation metadata.

3. Sign in as the Editor. The screening queue shows a manuscript awaiting
   screening; the editorial actions available are constrained by the manuscript's
   current lifecycle state, not by the interface hiding buttons.

4. Sign in as the Reviewer. The assigned manuscript is shown in its BLINDED form:
   no author name, no affiliation, no author identifier. This is enforced by the
   type the API returns, which has no author fields at all, rather than by
   filtering them out at the edge.

5. Sign in as the Author to view submissions and their status timelines.
